## JacobWare
This is how I imagined how somebody would make a game if they were making it only based on what i love the most, such as the beach, pikmin, sunny days, music and some more!!

## How to play?
The controls for the minigames are:
Minigame 1. Left and right keys to move, space to jump.
Minigame 2. Move and left click over the suns.
Minigame 3. Drag your mouse over the lamp.

Make sure to not run out of time!!
